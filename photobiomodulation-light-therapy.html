What is Photo Bio Modulation Therapy (PBMT)?

Photo Bio Modulation Therapy (PBMT) is a safe, non-invasive pain relief therapy that uses low-level light energy to help the body heal itself naturally.

In simple words: PBMT uses special therapeutic light (not heat, not radiation) that penetrates deep into the body tissues. This light stimulates your cells, improves blood flow, reduces inflammation, and relieves pain at its source. It is completely painless, does not involve injections, medicines, or surgery, and has no downtime.

This helps the body heal faster and function better.
